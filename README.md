## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst specializing in Business Intelligence, revenue analytics and data quality**, with **3+ years of combined experience in data and reporting operations** across Tata Consultancy Services (Vodafone project) and Recity.

At **TCS–Vodafone**, I supported telecom billing analysis, investigated discrepancies, developed Power BI reports and automated recurring Excel/Power Query workflows. My reported responsibilities include **approximately 500 billing records reviewed daily**, **about 10 hours saved per week** through reporting automation and daily reporting for **2–3 business teams**.

I also build hands-on portfolio projects connecting **SQL, Python, Snowflake, Power BI, n8n and local large language models (LLMs)**.

- 🎓 **Education:** MSc, Data Science & Analytics — **EPITA**, France (2025–2026)
- 📍 **Location:** Île-de-France, France
- 🎯 **Focus:** Data Analytics · Business Intelligence · Revenue Analytics · Data Automation
- 💼 **Interested in:** Data Analyst, BI Analyst and suitable internship opportunities
